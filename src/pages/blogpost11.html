DHS Plans $100M+ Follow-On OneStream Systems Integrator BPA

By Grant Deker · OneStream Solution Architect · Aug 26, 2026

The Department of Homeland Security plans to compete a follow-on blanket purchase agreement worth more than $100 million for a systems integrator to operate and maintain the department's OneStream software platform, according to a notice published on the department's Acquisition Planning Forecast System and reported by GovCon Wire. DHS expects to release the solicitation on October 20, with award anticipated in the second quarter of fiscal year 2027 and performance running through March 2032.

What DHS Announced

The BPA supports DHS's planning, programming, budgeting and execution (PPBE) work — the budgeting backbone of one of the largest departments in the federal government. The follow-on agreement will be awarded as a competitive combination-type contract, and it replaces the department's existing OneStream systems integrator arrangement. The scale is worth pausing on: a nine-figure, multi-year commitment not to license software, but to operate, maintain, and evolve a OneStream platform that is already in place.

Why It Matters Beyond Government

For finance and EPM leaders, this is a meaningful signal regardless of sector. DHS is doubling down on OneStream as the platform for department-wide planning and budgeting, committing to it into the next decade rather than re-competing the platform choice itself. It follows the pattern we see across large, complex organizations: consolidating planning, budgeting, consolidation, and reporting onto OneStream's unified platform rather than stitching together point solutions. It also caps a big year for OneStream — the company completed its approximately $6.4 billion go-private acquisition by Hg in April 2026 and continues to invest heavily in the platform and its partner ecosystem.

Our Take

Whether you are a federal agency or a commercial enterprise, the lesson in this announcement is the same: the value of a OneStream investment is realized in how well the platform is implemented, administered, and evolved over time. That is exactly why integrator and administration support commands this kind of sustained commitment — the platform decision is the beginning, not the end. If your organization is evaluating OneStream, migrating from a legacy EPM platform, or looking to get more out of an existing deployment, that operational discipline is where the return comes from.
